About

Three decades of Kentucky golf.

Bryan Conway won his first Kentucky Golf Association title in 1995, taking the Kentucky Amateur Championship at Kearney Hill Links as a member of The Players Club. Thirty-one years later, he's still adding to the record — a 2026 Kentucky Men's Senior Stroke Play Championship at Bardstown Country Club, and a deep run through that summer's Kentucky Senior Match Play Championship.

A University of Louisville golf alum, Conway played on the Canadian PGA Tour before returning to Kentucky's amateur ranks — where he plays out of the senior division today with the same game that made him a state champion in his twenties, still competing, still contending, still adding chapters to the record below.

Career

The Record

From back-to-back state championships to collegiate honors, a Kentucky Amateur title, professional golf and a championship resurgence more than three decades later, Bryan Conway's competitive record spans generations of Kentucky golf.

  • 1990
    KHSAA State Champion Champion
    Helped lead Franklin County to a Kentucky high school state golf championship, establishing himself as one of the state's premier young players.
  • 1991
    Back-to-Back KHSAA State Champion Champion
    Franklin County returned to the top the following season, giving Conway back-to-back state championships and cementing one of the defining chapters of his early career.
  • MSU
    Morehead State University — Collegiate Career
    Began his collegiate career at Morehead State and quickly established himself as one of the Ohio Valley Conference's top young golfers — named Freshman Player of the Year and finished third in the OVC Championship, then earned Most Valuable Player as a sophomore and First Team All-OVC honors in 1995.
  • 1995
    Kentucky Amateur Champion
    Captured the Kentucky Amateur Championship at Kearney Hill Links, representing The Players Club, with a score of 281 — one of the signature victories of his career, won while still competing collegiately.
  • UofL
    University of Louisville — Collegiate Career
    Transferred to the University of Louisville, continuing his collegiate career at another Division I program.
  • Pro
    The Professional Years — Canadian Tour
    Turned professional and pursued tournament golf at the highest level, competing through multiple trips to Canadian Tour Qualifying School as he pursued his professional career — the next chapter in a progression from Kentucky high school champion to decorated collegiate golfer, Kentucky Amateur champion and professional competitor.
  • 5×
    Frankfort City Champion
    Five-time Frankfort City Champion, adding victories in one of the region's longstanding competitive golf events.
  • 2×
    Daniel Boone Invitational Champion
    Two-time Daniel Boone Invitational Champion (1995 & 2018) — a title with a Conway family connection, as father Walter Conway won the same championship in 1968 during his own competitive career.
  • 2026
    The Resurgence
    More than three decades after his first state championships, Conway has again emerged as one of Kentucky's most competitive golfers. At 51 years old, he's turned the 2026 season into another championship chapter in a career that began at the highest levels of Kentucky golf.
  • 2026
    Kentucky Men's Senior Stroke Play Champion
    Won the Kentucky Golf Association Men's Senior Stroke Play Championship, July 27–28, at Bardstown Country Club — another statewide title more than 30 years after winning the Kentucky Amateur.
  • 2026
    Kentucky Men's Match Play — Senior Division Qualifying Medalist
    Led the Senior Division qualifying round at the 14th Clark's Pump-N-Shop Kentucky Men's Match Play Championship, Kearney Hill Golf Links — three-under 69 with an eagle on hole 3 and birdies on 9 and 11.
  • 2026
    Kentucky Senior Match Play — Semifinalist
    Won his opening match at the 15th Clark's Pump-N-Shop Kentucky Match Play Championship, Owensboro Country Club, 6&5, then beat David Horning 3&2 in the Quarterfinals before falling to G. Davis Boland 3&2 in the Senior Semifinals — a season of deep championship runs against Kentucky's top senior competition.
  • 2026
    Player of the Year Contender
    Conway's 2026 performance has placed him prominently in multiple Kentucky PGA Player of the Year races, including the senior standings, while also competing among players of all ages.
